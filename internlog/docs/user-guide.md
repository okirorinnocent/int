# User guide (foundation)

## Students

Register using a university email, confirm the verification email, then sign in. Your account remains pending until the institution's approval workflow activates it. Complete your profile when the student workspace is introduced.

## Employers

Register and await administrator verification. Do not publish opportunities until the employer record is verified.

## Workplace supervisors

Accounts must be assigned by an administrator to the relevant employer and placement. Only assigned interns should appear in the supervisor workspace.

## Academic supervisors

Accounts and student assignments are managed by the institution. Review access is limited to assigned students.

## Administrators

Create the first administrator only through the documented trusted SQL bootstrap. Keep privileged credentials private and review audit/security events regularly.
